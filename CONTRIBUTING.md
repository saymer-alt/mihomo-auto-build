# Contributing

**This repository is ARCHIVED and no longer maintained (since 2026-10-11).**

The MIPSel softfloat build experiment is finished by the owner's decision.
New pull requests, feature requests, and enhancement proposals are **not**
accepted, and no new builds or releases are planned. Historical releases,
source code, and research materials are preserved as-is for reference.

## If you still want to report something

- Do **not** open pull requests; the repository is archived and contributions
  cannot be integrated.
- Do not open issues asking for new builds, new architecture support, newer
  Mihomo versions, or revived maintenance — these are out of scope by the
  owner's decision.
- The only acceptable issue reports are non-sensitive factual corrections to
  the historical documentation; they may or may not be applied.

## Confidential security issues

**Do not post undisclosed vulnerabilities or sensitive exploit details in
public Issues or pull requests.** No verified private disclosure channel is
published here. The historical binaries should not be deployed anyway; rely
on actively maintained Mihomo packaging instead.
